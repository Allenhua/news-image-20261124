# Sources — Tuesday, 24 November 2026

Every story on the front page was verified as having happened, or first
been reported, on 24 November 2026. Stories from 23 November (Nvidia's
reinstatement of Jensen Huang, the COP31 collapse, the US Democrats'
House majority, FIFA's 2031/2035 Women's World Cup host announcement)
were deliberately left out.

1. **Korea — air-raid alert on Ulleung Island.** South Korea's government
   said a suspected North Korean aircraft breached its airspace near
   Ulleung Island on Tuesday, ordering residents to shelters; the alert
   was lifted within minutes. AP / Yonhap, via Times of India live blog,
   24 Nov 2026.
2. **Ukraine war — Russian strikes on drone sites in Sumy.** The Russian
   military struck drone control sites, Starlink terminals and other
   assets in Sumy region, Interfax reported, citing the defence ministry;
   deadly Russian strikes also hit the Kyiv region. Times of India live
   blog / SCMP, 24 Nov 2026.
3. **Space — NATO vows space defence.** NATO vows space defence after a
   report that Russia is planning a launch within weeks that would give
   it the ability to destroy satellites; allies say Russia may launch a
   nuclear space weapon next month. SCMP / The Japan Times, 24 Nov 2026.
4. **United States — Trump halts Iowa detonation.** Trump halted the
   planned detonation of explosive material at an Iowa plant, pending a
   court ruling. Times of India live blog, 24 Nov 2026.
5. **Bangladesh — Hasina plans her return.** Ousted PM Sheikh Hasina
   plans to return home from more than two years of exile in India on
   16 December, the anniversary of the 1971 liberation war. Reuters,
   24 Nov 2026.
6. **Pakistan — magnitude 4.1 earthquake.** An earthquake of magnitude
   4.1 struck Pakistan in the morning, according to the National Centre
   for Seismology. Times of India live blog, 24 Nov 2026.
